### If you generate your own art from another mod

`tools/convert_cursor.py` will happily convert artwork extracted from any cursor
replacer, and that is fine for personal use. Do **not** redistribute the result
unless that mod's permissions allow it. Most do not. Ship a patch that tells users
to supply their own art instead.
